## What changed

The desktop tray (`mcc-desktop`) and RTK (`mcc-rtk`) commands were declared in `pyproject.toml` but never surfaced to users:

- The installers neither verified their shims nor mentioned them in the post-install message.
- `mcc-help` omitted `mcc-desktop`.

Fixes:
- `install.sh` + `install.ps1`: add `mcc-rtk` + `mcc-desktop` to the verification loop and the post-install command reference.
- `entrypoints.py` (`mcc-help`): list `mcc-desktop` alongside the other commands.
- Test fixtures: command lists now include both; `test_cli_scripts_are_registered` now asserts the `gui-scripts` (`mcc-desktop`/`fcc-desktop`) and `mcc-rtk` are registered.
