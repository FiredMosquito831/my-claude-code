Every reviewed screen, grouped

These are the product's own captures, reviewed one by one before publication. Each was checked for keys, tokens, account names, file paths and real prompt text; where a capture carried something that did not belong on a public page, it was redacted on a copy and the original was left untouched. Every image below has a caption, and every caption describes the state the screen is actually in.

The screens are grouped by the area of the product they belong to, in the same order as the features: the dashboard itself, providers and credentials, routing, reasoning and limits, observability and cost, web search, agents, media and messaging. A capture appears once, in the group where it is most useful.

How the screens were reviewed

Every tracked image in the repository was inventoried, hashed and de-duplicated first. The survivors were reviewed on contact sheets and, where a leak was plausible, at full resolution. The verdicts were include, redact or exclude with a reason. Redactions are solid rectangles applied to a copy, and each rectangle is recorded with its coordinates. The excluded captures are the ones that were not product screens, or that could not be cleaned without destroying what they showed.
