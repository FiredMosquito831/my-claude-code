The AGPL option

The AGPL option is free and open source, and it is open to everyone. Personal use, study, modification, and commercial use are all permitted at no cost, including running the proxy for your own work or for your employer's work. What the AGPL asks in return is source code: if you convey the software, or run a modified version of it as a network service that other people interact with, you must offer those users the complete corresponding source of your version under the same licence. Section 13, Remote Network Interaction, is the clause that extends that duty to network use.

The Required Notice at the top of the licence file must be preserved. That is a term of the AGPL grant, stated under the additional term of AGPL section 7(b):

https://github.com/FiredMosquito831/my-claude-code

The commercial option

The commercial licence releases you from the AGPL's source-sharing obligation. It is the right choice if you want to offer the software, or a work based on it, as a hosted or managed service; route model or API traffic commercially through it as part of a paid product; reuse its code, its routing logic or its algorithms inside a product you ship without publishing that product's source under the AGPL; or distribute it inside a closed-source appliance, gateway or bundle.

A commercial licence is the only way to use the software outside the terms of the AGPL. There is no other exception, standing permission or informal allowance. Terms are negotiated case by case: scope, duration, support expectations and price depend on what you are building. There is no published price list and no click-through agreement; every commercial licence is a written agreement with the author.
